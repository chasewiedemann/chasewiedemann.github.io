# About

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Business Economics at Olin Business School, Washington University in St. Louis, and a candidate on the 2026-2027 academic job market.

I study how local government organization shapes public-good provision and regional economic growth. My research combines public finance questions with tools from industrial organization.
