I am a PhD candidate in economics at Olin Business School, Washington University in St. Louis. My research focuses on local public finance, urban economics, and political economy.

I study how the organization of local governments affects public goods and the growth of U.S. metropolitan areas. In particular, I am interested in the tradeoffs between coordination across jurisdictions and competition among them.

My current work examines local government centralization and metropolitan growth.
